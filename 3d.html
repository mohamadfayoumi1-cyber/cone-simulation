Collaborative cone swarm – decentralized simulation

The operator sends one command to one cone (the agent). The agent shares it, every cone bids for a position, they agree on roles, and each cone then drives itself using GPS. Break a cone and the rest notice, re-plan and redraw the shape. Drag to rotate, scroll or pinch to zoom.
